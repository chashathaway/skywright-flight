# Changelog

## 0.1.0-preupload

First share-prep build.

- Trainer aircraft with server-side flight physics
- Chase camera with visible aircraft pitch/roll
- Bank-and-pull turning
- Crash detection against terrain, trees, blocks, and aircraft
- Aircraft explosions with salvage drops
- Plane-shaped ground shadow
- Missiles with weak homing and large explosions
- Ejection and parachute descent
- 12 persistent player home runways around origin
- Runway lights and manual runway repair commands
- Procedural hills, trees, flowers, and light biomes
- Basic on-foot digging and crafting
- Wood, sticks, tools, stove, glass, iron/steel, and craftable trainer kit
- Titanium and coal materials with ore generation, crafting, tools, and missile-resistant armor
- In-game flight manual
- HUD flight information and configurable flight tuning console
